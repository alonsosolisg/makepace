Privacy

Last updated · 24 October 2026

MakePace is operated by Alonso Solis, who is responsible for the personal information handled to operate this website and respond to enquiries. For privacy questions, contact alonsosolisgerloni@gmail.com.

This website

The landing page has no sign-up form, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Fonts and artwork are served with the site. Our host, Vercel, processes request information such as IP addresses, browser information and requested URLs to deliver and secure the website. See Vercel's privacy notice.

The desktop application

Workspaces, conversations and generated files are stored locally. Integration credentials are stored using the operating system's encryption facilities. Uninstalling the application does not automatically erase your workspace data or backups. You can locate these through the app's workspace and app-data folder commands.

Services you connect

Local storage does not mean offline processing. Codex receives prompts and context needed for your requests. Apify receives collection requests when you use that connection. OneUp receives content, media and scheduling details when you use publishing features. Websites opened during research also receive network requests. Those services handle data under their own terms and privacy notices.

The update checker contacts GitHub to check releases; it does not upload your workspace content. Downloads, repository visits and issue reports are handled by GitHub. Do not put credentials or private customer information in public issues.

Purpose, retention and your choices

Website request processing supports delivery, reliability and abuse prevention, based on our legitimate interest in operating a secure website. Vercel manages infrastructure logs under its retention policies; retention depends on the type of record and operational, security and legal needs. We do not maintain a separate visitor analytics database.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request, based on our legitimate interest in supporting the project, or a legal obligation when handling statutory requests. Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the matter and meet applicable legal obligations or handle related claims. Please do not send passwords or API keys.

Hosting, email and connected-service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their privacy notices explain applicable transfer safeguards and how to request more information. See Vercel's notice and Google's notice for website hosting and email respectively. Connected accounts are also governed by your arrangements with those providers.

You control local workspace files and can delete them and their backups. Disconnect integrations and revoke credentials with the relevant provider when you no longer need them. Connected providers have their own retention policies; deleting a local file does not delete copies already sent to them.

Privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, obtain portable data, and complain to your data-protection authority. Email alonsosolisgerloni@gmail.com to make a request. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month; any permitted extension will be explained. Do not submit personal privacy requests in public GitHub issues.
