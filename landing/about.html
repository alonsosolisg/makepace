A PROCESS YOU CAN REPEAT

From “what works?”
to “ready to post.”

Choose a step to see what to ask for
and what to review.

01Find a direction+

Start with your audience and study original posts. Separate a repeatable format from the topic it happens to cover.

“Find formats for our audience. Show me the original posts and what the visible results actually support.”

Review: source links, audience fit and missing evidence.

02Build the batch+

Choose formats and different angles, then ask for complete stories instead of disconnected hooks.

“Make six posts across these two formats. Give each a distinct angle and show me the full sequence.”

Review: story, usefulness and whether each post stands on its own.

03Get specific with feedback+

Inspect your output alongside the conversation. Point to the slide, line or image that needs work and ask for the next version.

“The second slide doesn't follow the hook. Make the situation clearer and keep the image sequence consistent.”

Review: readability, crops, visual continuity and product claims.

04Approve, publish, learn+

Connect OneUp to prepare publishing. Check exact posts, accounts and times before approving. Use collected results to choose what to test next.

“Prepare a schedule for this approved batch. Show me the destinations and times before publishing.”

Review: account, caption, media, timing and platform requirements.

BEFORE YOU START

Open source.
Still an alpha.

What stays on my computer?

Workspace files, conversations and generated assets are stored locally. Prompts and selected context go to the services you use. Local does not mean offline AI. See our privacy notice.

What does it cost?

MakePace is free under Apache 2.0. Codex, Apify and OneUp have their own account requirements, terms and possible charges.

What should I know before downloading?

Windows, macOS and Linux installers are available. They are currently unsigned, and Mac builds are not notarized. Read the GitHub installation guidance and back up important work. The alpha still needs real-world workflow testing.

Are these screenshots real?

Yes. These are captures of the Hey Mom workspace in browser development mode, with an empty conversation and disconnected integrations. The desktop app uses the same interface with native connection and credential storage support. They are not evidence of a completed publishing run.
