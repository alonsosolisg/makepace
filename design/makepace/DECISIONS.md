# MakePace identity decisions

- Name: **MakePace**, with capital M and P.
- Palette: **3A · Porcelain** — solid turquoise `#007D7B`, canvas `#F5F2EB`, cards `#FFFDFA`, warm tint `#ECE8DF`, ink `#233D3B`.
- Logo: **09 · Batch cadence**, selected by the user. Editable source: `logos/09-batch-cadence.svg`.
- Interface icons: **Phosphor**, replacing the earlier Iconoir request. Use Regular as the starting weight at 20–24 px. Source: https://phosphoricons.com/
- Typography: **Manrope**, approved. Wordmark 700, headings and controls 600, body 400–500; tabular numerals for quantitative data. The app self-hosts the variable font with its OFL license.
- The working studio at `/studio` uses this identity. Hey Mom remains its content workspace; historical batch assets and publishing records keep their original branding.
